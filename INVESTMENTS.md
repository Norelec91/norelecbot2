# Investments

The bank is closed. `We @YourTelegramUsername N` (or `We YourIrcNick N` on IRC)
now only answers that investments no longer exist, and `We @YourTelegramUsername`
is just the way home again.

When the version that closed the bank first loaded the game state, every deposit
went back to its owner's score: the palle put in, without the returns the days
had made of them. The `investments`, `investment_magnitudes` and
`lightning_history` sections were dropped from `conquister.json`. What each
player got back was kept under `bank_refunds` until the raid keeper, on its
second round, told the group once, mentioning the Telegram players, and emptied
the list.
